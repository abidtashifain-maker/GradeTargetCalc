# Calculator article standard

Owner request: all 30 tools receive 1,100–1,300 words of original English educational article content, short boxed sections and appropriate infographics.

The calculator stays before the article. Each article contains an accessible worked-example flow diagram, clearly marked as an example rather than a live result, topic navigation, short paragraphs, formulas, worked scenarios, limitations and at least four FAQs. Visual diagrams are HTML/CSS rather than heavy images so labels remain readable on mobile and available to assistive technology. Content is statically rendered and available without JavaScript.

Word counts include article headings, visual labels, paragraphs, lists and FAQ answers. Shared navigation, calculator controls, footer and related links are excluded. The build refuses an out-of-range article. The editorial audit independently counts rendered HTML.

## Policy review

Official Google guidance consulted 2 November 2026:

- https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/7299563 — original useful content, inviting layout and usable navigation.
- https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/48182 — program policies and nondeceptive navigation.
- https://support.google.com/publisherpolicies/answer/11112688 — publisher value; review and curation of generated material.
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content — people-first content; no preferred word count.

The word range is the owner’s editorial specification, not a Google approval threshold. These educational pages do not claim official school affiliation, standardized grade conversions, author qualifications or guaranteed outcomes. No ad code, ad-click prompts, tracking or consent changes are introduced by this update. Advertising, if added later, needs a separate placement and applicable privacy/consent review. AdSense approval and changes in engagement are not guaranteed.
